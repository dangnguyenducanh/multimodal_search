# Phase 4: Reranker training (text)

**Goal:** one text reranker that beats ZR's best single member and, ideally, ZR's whole 5-model ensemble, at a
fraction of its cost.
**Baseline:** the ZR run from [0.md](0.md): members c1–c3 (4-class) and r1–r2 (regression).
**Sources:** [ZR] `code_task1|2/run_glue_no_trainer.py` (loop), `RobertaWithSampleWeight*.py` (models), `utils.py`
(`EMA`, `FGM`, `util_loss_fct`), `run_train.sh` · [www] paper §3.2–3.3 + Table 2 (step-by-step gains, no code).

## Their method [ZR]
| Part | What they did |
|---|---|
| Model | cross-encoder `tokenizer(query, product_text)` → XLM-R/InfoXLM-large (their domain-pretrained weights) → [CLS] + 128-d brand and color ID embeddings → dense → head |
| Heads | **4-class** E/S/C/I with cross-entropy, ranked by `gain(argmax) × max prob`; **regression** on the gain (1 / 0.1 / 0.01 / 0) with MSE. Both weighted per row by the sample weight |
| Tricks (every step) | forward → loss → backward → **FGM** attack on word embeddings (ε 0.5) → adversarial loss → backward → **R-Drop** KL(adv, clean) × 0.5 → restore → optimizer step → **EMA** (0.999) |
| Settings | length 256 padded, lr 1e-5, effective batch 256, 10 epochs, warmup 2% (hard-coded), fp32; 4-class loss = Poly-1 cross-entropy |
| Selection | validate with EMA weights every 847 (regression) / 518 (4-class) optimizer steps, keep the best |
| Variant | `RobertaWithSampleWeight_Bag`: adds char 3-gram bags + a locale embedding to the brand/color IDs beside [CLS] |
| Their numbers (public LB) | single regressor ≈ 0.900; [www]'s best single for comparison: 0.9022 |

## Their weaknesses
| # | Weakness | Why it matters |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | A multilingual backbone for an English target | [www]'s English DeBERTa-v3-large single model (0.9022) beat ZR's single (≈ 0.900) |
| 2 | Pointwise losses (CE, MSE). The 4-class score `gain(argmax) × prob` throws away probability mass: P(E) = 0.45, P(S) = 0.55 scores 0.055, although the expected gain is 0.505 | nDCG ranks lists within a query, and the training signal and scoring rule don't match it |
| 3 | [www]'s measured gains are missing: self-distillation + pseudo-labels (+0.0022), label smoothing (+0.0008), AWP + DeBERTa-v3-large (+0.0032), multi-sample dropout and weighted layer pooling | known, measured improvements left on the table |
| 4 | FGM and R-Drop roughly double the cost of every step, and **neither was measured alone** | possibly paying 2× for nothing |
| 5 | Wasted compute: fixed 256-token padding, 10 epochs, validation on ~400k rows every 0.1 epoch | high GPU-hours per model, multiplied by 9 models in their recipe |
| 6 | Validation interval hard-coded in steps (847 / 518), tied to their file sizes | it silently changes meaning with any other data |
| 7 | The checkpoint is the best of many noisy validation readings, with no CI | an upward-biased pick; unknown whether it is really better |

## Our target
- **Single model vs single model:** beat ZR's best member (r1 from Phase 0) on dev, with CI > 0.
- **Single model vs ZR ensemble:** match or beat the 5-member ZR baseline with **one** model, which is about 5× cheaper
  at inference. A loss here is reported as is; Phase 6 then closes the gap.
- Report GPU-hours per model next to ZR's.

## Our proposal

### 4.0 Control: our reimplementation of their recipe, and the proxy scale
- `mmsearch/train.py` reimplements ZR's loop (heads, FGM, R-Drop, EMA, sample weights) from their code, citing file
  and line. Their code has no license, so it is not copied.
- **Proxy scale** (used by Phases 1–5 to test changes cheaply): `xlm-roberta-base`, Task 1 US train rows, 1 epoch,
  length 256, their regression head (their strongest single). Validation on dev every 0.1 epoch.
- **Parity:** at proxy scale, their trainer (the Phase 0 working copy) vs ours, same data and seed. The dev nDCG
  difference must have a CI that includes 0 and be within ±0.003. Only then is "**ZR recipe @ proxy**" the control that
  every change is measured against.

### 4.1 Improvement ladder (proxy scale; one change at a time, kept only if dev improves with CI > 0)
| # | Change vs ZR | Fixes | Source | Their reported gain |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 0 | speed: dynamic padding, length-sorted batches, bf16, validation on the 2,000 dev queries | 5, 6 | [ours], ETS-Lab | must not lose: CI lower bound > −0.002 |
| 1 | scoring: 4-class with **expected gain** `P(E) + 0.1·P(S) + 0.01·P(C)` vs their argmax rule vs regression; and both heads on one encoder (CE + MSE, scores summed) | 2 | [www], 3rd place, [ours] | — |
| 2 | **listwise loss:** softmax cross-entropy over each query's judged products (batches grouped by query), alone and added to the pointwise loss | 2 | [ours] | — |
| 3 | **ablate their tricks:** remove FGM, R-Drop, EMA one at a time and keep only those that pay for their cost | 4 | [ours] | not reported alone |
| 4 | AWP instead of FGM, started once the loss is below 0.6 | 3 | [www] §3.3.6 | +0.0032 together with step 10 |
| 5 | label smoothing ε 0.1 | 3 | [www] §3.3 | 0.8982 → 0.8990 |
| 6 | self-distillation: 3-fold out-of-fold predictions on train → target `0.7·onehot + 0.3·oof_probs` | 3, and Phase 2 weakness 6 | [www] §3.3.1 | 0.8960 → 0.8982 with step 7 |
| 7 | pseudo-labels, legit version: score **unjudged** products retrieved for **train** queries (Phase 7), keep max prob > 0.7, soft targets, weight 0.3. Never test queries | 3 | [www] §3.3.2, adapted | see 6 |
| 8 | multi-sample dropout (5 masks) + learned weighted pooling over the last layers' [CLS] | 3 | [www] §3.3.3 | part of the 0.9059 public single |
| 9 | side features: ablate their brand/color ID embeddings (in every member), then add their `_Bag` char 3-gram bags + locale, which their final ensemble didn't use | — | [ZR] | not reported |
| 10 | backbone: `deberta-v3-base` at proxy, then `deberta-v3-large` at full scale | 1 | [www] | best [www] single 0.9022 public |
| 11 | domain-pretrained start (Phase 3 winner, if any) | — | [ZR] | +0.0008 (Task 2) |
| — | not taken: prompt tuning and attention-over-attention ([www] §3.3.4–5, no gain numbers given); Poly-1 / focal / GHM losses ([ZR] used Poly-1 only for Tasks 2/3) | | | |

### 4.2 Full scale
The winning recipe on the full train pool (Phase 1 decision) with the winning large backbone, for 1–2 epochs (stop
when dev flattens). Compared on dev with ZR r1 (single vs single) and the ZR 5-member ensemble; the winner goes to
final test once. Its out-of-fold predictions on train are kept for Phases 5 and 6.

## Eval
- **Primary:** dev nDCG, paired with the previous rung (ladder) or with ZR (4.2).
- **Secondary**, from the same model: Task 2 micro-F1 and Task 3 substitute F1 on dev, both vs ZR's from Phase 0.
- **Per run:** GPU-hours, inference ms per query (20 candidates), peak VRAM, all next to ZR's numbers.

## Output
`mmsearch/model.py` (`CrossEncoder`, heads, side-feature block), `mmsearch/train.py` (loop, FGM/AWP, R-Drop, EMA,
listwise loss, self-distillation targets), `scripts/p4_train_reranker.py`, checkpoints and out-of-fold predictions in `runs/p4/`.
