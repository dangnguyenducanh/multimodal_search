# Phase 3: Domain pretraining (decided by evidence, may be dropped)

**Goal:** find out whether adapting the backbone to Amazon product language before fine-tuning is worth its GPU-hours.
**Baseline:** the ZR run from [0.md](0.md). It started from vanilla `xlm-roberta-large`, because ZR never released
their pretrained checkpoint. **Sources:** [ZR] paper §3.1 + Table 3; `code_pretrain/multi_task_model.py`,
`run_mlm_no_trainer_multi_task*.py`, `run_train_pretrain_multi_task.sh`, `code_preprocess/3_create_pretrain_data*.py`.
2nd place also continued pretraining on competition text; [www] did not pretrain.

## Their method [ZR]
| Part | What they did |
|---|---|
| Data | text of all catalog products (~1.8M), with the public/private **test** products oversampled ×2 → `Product_shuffled_220W.csv` (≈ 2.2M rows) |
| Masked words | 15% of tokens masked, max 256 tokens, LM head |
| Product2Query | a random snippet of a product's text acts as a fake query (length ~ Poisson, mean 4 words EN/ES, 8 JP); a 2-class head says whether it belongs to that product; **Poly-1 loss** `ε·(1−p_t) + CE` |
| Product2Brand / Product2Color | predict the product's brand / color ID from its text (CE, unknown = ignore) |
| Contrastive | SimCSE-style with a MoCo queue, temperature 0.03 |
| Settings | start `xlm-roberta-large` (paper: InfoXLM-large), batch 8 × 16, lr 2e-5, 10 epochs |
| Their result (Table 3, Task 2 CV micro-F1 × 100) | masked words 74.97 → + Product2Query **75.05** → + brand/color 75.08 → + contrastive 75.08 |

## Their weaknesses
| # | Weakness | Why it matters |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | The measured gain is tiny (+0.11 F1 points in total, +0 from contrastive) and was **never measured on Task 1 nDCG** | it's unknown whether it moves the metric we care about |
| 2 | 10 epochs of a large model over 2.2M texts | likely more GPU-hours than any fine-tuning run |
| 3 | Test products were oversampled ×2. No labels were used, but the method is transductive | a deployed system wouldn't have the test catalog in advance |
| 4 | The checkpoint was never released | Phase 0 starts from vanilla weights, so their pretraining is the one part of their recipe we haven't measured yet |
| 5 | It adapts a multilingual backbone for a US-English target | a better English backbone (Phase 4 step 10) may give more for less |
| 6 | Product2Query uses random snippets as fake queries, while 74,888 real train queries with labelled products go unused | real queries look different from snippets |

## Our target
A pretrained start must beat a vanilla start of the **same** Phase 4 recipe on dev (paired CI > 0), and its gain per
GPU-hour must be at least that of the cheapest Phase 4 ladder step it competes with. Otherwise the phase is dropped
and RESULTS.md records why. A drop is a valid result.

## Our proposal (at proxy scale, [4.md](4.md) §4.0)
| # | Variant | Fixes | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Their method on our data:** their `code_pretrain` scripts (working copy + path edits, as in Phase 0) on the proxy backbone, 1 epoch, **without** test oversampling | 1, 3, 4 | [ZR] |
| 2 | **Cheap version:** masked words + Product2Query only (the only task that showed a gain), 1 epoch | 2 | [ZR] reduced |
| 3 | **Real queries:** Product2Query positives from (train query, Exact product) pairs, negatives from Irrelevant pairs, plus the snippet task. It uses train labels only and never touches dev/test | 6 | [ours] |
| 4 | Whichever of 1–3 wins, applied to the Phase 4 backbone winner (e.g. DeBERTa-v3) with our `mmsearch/pretrain.py` | 5 | [ours] |
| — | not taken: contrastive MoCo (no gain in their Table 3); test-product oversampling | | |

Settings: lr 2e-5, length 256, batch sized to the VRAM free at launch minus ~3 GB, epochs 1 → 2 only while the
Phase 4 gain still rises.

## Eval
- **Sanity:** held-out masked-word loss falls; Product2Query accuracy on held-out products > 90%.
- **Decision:** fine-tune the Phase 4 proxy recipe twice, from vanilla and from pretrained weights, with the same
  seed and data → paired bootstrap on dev. Log the GPU-hours for pretraining and fine-tuning separately.
- If a variant wins at proxy scale, repeat once on the full-scale backbone before Phase 4's final model uses it.

`mmsearch/pretrain.py` (data builder + multi-task heads), `scripts/p3_pretrain.py`, checkpoints in `runs/p3/`.
