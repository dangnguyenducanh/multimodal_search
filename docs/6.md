# Phase 6: Ensemble

**Goal:** beat ZR's ensemble, both on score and on cost per query.
**Baseline:** the ZR 5-member ensemble from [0.md](0.md), whose per-member outputs are saved in
`runs/p0/member_scores.parquet`. **Sources:** [ZR] `code_evaluation/task1/run_origin.py` (members and `sort_key_4`, with
public-LB scores in comments) · [www] paper §3.4 + Table 2 · 3rd place §2.5 · ETS-Lab (LightGBM over cross-encoder outputs).

## Their method [ZR]
| Member | Head | Product text order |
|---|---|---|
| c1 | 4-class | title · brand · color · bullets · desc |
| c2 | 4-class | title · bullets · desc · brand · color |
| c3 | 4-class | title · desc · bullets · brand · color |
| r1 | regression on gain | default |
| r2 | regression on gain | default, trained after the confident-learning filter |

```text
p          = mean(probs_c1, probs_c2, probs_c3)          # 4-class probabilities, averaged
label_gain = {E: 1.0, S: 0.1, C: 0.01, I: 0.0}[argmax(p)]
score      = label_gain × max(p)  +  mean(reg_r1, reg_r2)  # sort_key_4
```
Their public-LB log: c1+c2+c3 = 0.8946 · single regressor ≈ 0.900 · all 5 = **0.9032**.
For comparison, [www] (1st place) averaged **different model families** (DeBERTa-v3-large, InfoXLM, XLM-R, RemBERT,
mDeBERTa) with weights chosen from cross-validation and correlation: best single 0.9022 → 0.9057 public.

## Their weaknesses
| # | Weakness | Why it matters |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | `gain(argmax) × max prob` throws away probability mass (P(E) = 0.45, P(S) = 0.55 → 0.055, though the expected gain is 0.505) | products near the E/S boundary are ranked as if they were Substitutes |
| 2 | Classifier and regressor scores are **added on raw, different scales**, with no weights | one part can dominate by accident of scale |
| 3 | Equal weights, never fitted | members that are worse or redundant count as much as the best |
| 4 | All diversity comes from field order and head type in **one model family** | members make similar mistakes; [www] got its ensemble gain from different families |
| 5 | 5 large models at inference, no pruning, members loaded one after another | about 5× the cost of a single model per query, with no curve of gain per millisecond |
| 6 | No images in any member | see [5.md](5.md) |

## Our target
- **Score:** beat the ZR baseline on dev (paired CI > 0), then confirm on final test.
- **Cost:** report the ensemble's ms per query next to ZR's. If two options tie on score, the cheaper one wins. Also
  report the smallest ensemble that matches ZR's score.

## Our proposal: one change at a time from the ZR baseline
Each stage stays only if it beats the previous stage on dev (paired bootstrap). Its added latency is logged.

| Stage | Change | Fixes | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0 | **ZR baseline:** `zr_score()` over Phase 0's saved member outputs. It must reproduce their ranking file exactly ([0.md](0.md) §0.6). No retraining | — | [ZR] |
| 1 | expected gain `Σ gain·mean(p)` + mean regression, both rank-normalized per query | 1, 2 | [www], 3rd place |
| 2 | fitted weights: coordinate ascent on dev nDCG, 5-fold CV over dev queries; correlated members down-weighted | 3 | [www] |
| 3 | **family diversity:** add our Phase 4 models (DeBERTa-v3-large, and others the ladder produced) next to or instead of ZR members | 4 | [www] |
| 4 | seed/fold bagging of the strongest member | 4 | [www] ("2 of 7 folds") |
| 5 | image members from Phase 5 (variant B or C rerankers, or the variant A combiner) | 6 | [ours] |
| 6 | stacking: replace the formula with a learned combiner over all member outputs (LR → LightGBM / XGBoost, the same ladder and GroupKFold procedure as [5.md](5.md)) | 2, 3 | ETS-Lab |
| 7 | prune: greedy backward removal, dropping members whose leave-one-out gain CI includes 0 or whose gain per ms is lowest | 5 | [ours] |

### The five ways two members can differ (why an ensemble helps)
| Axis | Who used it | Why it helps |
|---|---|---|
| backbone family | [www] yes (main source); [ZR] no | different pretraining data and tokenizers → different errors; usually the largest gain |
| field order | [ZR] yes (c1/c2/c3) | at 256 tokens the order decides which fields survive, so each member reads different text |
| head | [ZR] yes (c vs r) | classification and regression rank differently |
| seed / fold | [www] yes; [ZR] fold variants | cheapest; reduces variance |
| image use | nobody (no images in 2022) | new information rather than a new view of the same text |

Every member's per-pair outputs are stored once (`runs/p6/member_scores.parquet`, starting from Phase 0's), so
combining and pruning need no GPU.

## Eval
- Each stage vs the previous one and vs the ZR baseline on dev, paired bootstrap. Fitted combiners are scored out of
  fold (5-fold CV over dev queries).
- Per member: leave-one-out gain, correlation matrix of per-pair scores, inference ms per query.
- A **gain per extra millisecond** curve; the serving budget in [9.md](9.md) picks the point on it.
- Final test once, for the winning stage. RESULTS.md shows ZR baseline → each stage → final.

## Output
`mmsearch/ensemble.py` (`zr_score`, expected-gain formula, weighted mean, greedy prune, stacking hook),
`scripts/p6_ensemble.py`, `runs/p6/`.
